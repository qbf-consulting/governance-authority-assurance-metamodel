# v0.9.2 — Ecosystem Validation Evidence

{% include gaam-meta.html %}

**Release date:** 22 September 2026  
**Normative baseline:** v0.9.0

GAAM v0.9.2 is a candidate maintenance release that converts selected findings from the paired EUDI Wallet ARF v3.0.0 and GLEIF vLEI EGF v4.0 implementation-validation cases into executable behavioural evidence.

It does **not** modify GAAM v0.9.0 normative requirements, profile identifiers, canonical schema identifiers, governed vocabulary semantics or conformance levels.

## Material changes

- Add five behavioural vectors derived from recurring EUDI/vLEI findings:
  - reject role or recognition evidence when the requested effect is outside authority scope;
  - reject a dependent delegation when upstream authority is inactive or revoked;
  - treat unknown or stale runtime authority state as safe only when the effect fails closed;
  - reject assurance evidence outside its validity window; and
  - reject consequential decision evidence that lacks an attributable accountable party.
- Map every added vector into requirement-level assurance traceability so no new test is orphaned.
- Carry forward the ecosystem-crosswalk schema v1.1 and paired explicit-authority / distributed-authority benchmark method introduced after v0.9.1.
- Advance the portable conformance-kit and result-contract distribution identity to v0.9.2 while retaining the v0.9.0 normative version.
- Regenerate package and validation evidence under the v0.9.2 distribution identity.

## Source validation cases

The added vectors are bounded tests of **existing GAAM v0.9.0 semantics**. They originate from implementation questions exposed by:

- the EUDI Wallet ARF case around action-level authority, registry semantics, distributed accountability, continuous assurance and fail-safe runtime state; and
- the vLEI EGF case around role versus transaction authority, delegation dependency, assurance freshness and relying-party accountability.

The external ecosystems are not being assessed for GAAM conformance. Their reviews are used to pressure-test GAAM's existing model.

## Compatibility

Existing v0.9.0 conforming artifacts remain valid. Implementations continue to identify `gaamVersion: 0.9.0`, use v0.9.0 profile identifiers and resolve canonical schemas under the v0.9.0 namespace.

`0.9.2` identifies the maintenance distribution, portable tooling contract, behavioural test-suite state and generated package evidence.

## Assurance boundary

The new vectors demonstrate repository-controlled and portable test behaviour against the existing GAAM semantics. They do not constitute independent implementation evidence, external interoperability evidence or certification, and they do not close GAAM's v1.0.0 independent-evidence gates.
