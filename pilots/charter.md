# Pilot Charter Template

Copy these fields into a pilot-specific, version-controlled record. **Blank values are not approval.** A charter is a governance and evidence-planning artifact, not a claim of GAAM conformance.

| Field | Pilot participant fills in |
| --- | --- |
| Pilot identifier and version | Stable ID, date, owner, review history |
| Sponsor and competent authority | Who authorizes the test, exact basis and scope of authority |
| Implementer, operator, independent reviewer | Named roles and relationships; conflicts disclosed |
| Governance problem and falsifiable hypothesis | What effect is governed, why, and what would invalidate the model |
| GAAM baseline, conformance target and profiles | Version, exact declared profiles, dependencies and applicable requirement IDs |
| Intended effects and affected parties | Test resource, risk, rights, notice, challenge and remedy |
| Controls and authoritative evidence | Policy source, delegated scope, status authority, freshness, precedence, provenance |
| Pilot data and environment | Synthetic/approved dataset, access boundary, permitted integrations and retention |
| Success and stop conditions | Positive, invalid scope, revoked parent, stale/unavailable evidence, unknown execution outcome |
| Gate owners and evidence references | G0–G6 evidence, independent verification plan, exact commit/build IDs and timestamps |
| Residual risks and production exclusions | Known gaps and incident escalation owner |
| Closure authority and disposition | Who can sign off; continuation, termination, escalation or remediation |

## Decision before G0

The sponsor explicitly approves the pilot scope and identifies the accountable governance owner. If no competent authority can be identified, record the issue and do **not** proceed to a live governed effect. An internal demonstration may use declared fictitious authority only if it clearly says that the demonstration is synthetic.

## Pilot hypothesis example

A narrowly delegated actor may update an illustrative record **only** within the agreed effect and period. Out-of-scope grants, revoked parent authority, or stale and unavailable required status must not silently authorize a mutation. The decision receipt must be distinguishable from evidence that the requested mutation actually took effect.

This sample is informative. It does not imply that the sample institutional authority exists.
