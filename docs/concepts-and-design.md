# Concepts and Design

Understand the metamodel architecture, design principles, diagrams, and rationale before applying normative requirements.

For an entry point organized around questions rather than files, use [Find a diagram by governance question](../diagrams/architecture-diagrams.md#find-a-diagram-by-governance-question). The guide groups the existing visual views around **authority and delegation**, **governance decisions and observed effects**, and **evidence and assurance**. Each route connects to an existing example and its evidence limit; it introduces no additional authority model or conformance claim.
