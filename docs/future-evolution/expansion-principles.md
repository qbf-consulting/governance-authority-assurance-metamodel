# Expansion Principles

Future GAAM development follows six controls.

1. **Evidence before normativity.** A concept is tested through guidance, prototypes, patterns, and behavioural evidence before promotion.
2. **Small core, specialised profiles.** Universally reusable semantics belong in the core; domain-specific obligations belong in profiles.
3. **Distinct predicates remain distinct.** Identity, standing, qualification, role, authority, authorisation, execution, and accountability must not collapse into one signal.
4. **Lifecycle and failure are first-class.** Every candidate concept must address transition, suspension, revocation, succession, degradation, and closure where relevant.
5. **Machine actionability follows conceptual stability.** Experimental schemas may prototype concepts, but cannot define conformance until independently exercised.
6. **Affected-party and market effects count.** Expansion must consider people outside the immediate trust community, systemic concentration, and remedy propagation.

Promotion requires implementation evidence, review, interoperability analysis, and an explicit release-impact decision.
